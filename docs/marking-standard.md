# UPSC Mains marking standard for the SatyaDheesh grader

This is the standard the grader is built from. It states, for each criterion, what the evidence says, how the grader measures it, and what it caps. UPSC publishes no rubric or mark bands, so every rule below is a constructed standard built from UPSC's printed instructions, candidates' reported scores and the coaching institutes' published rubrics, in that order of weight.

## What the evidence can and cannot support

The only official statements are the instructions printed on the question paper. The 2024 GS II paper says there are twenty compulsory questions and that "Word limit in questions should be adhered to" [[1](https://www.vajiraoinstitute.com/assets/study-materials/pdfs/upsc-pyq/archive/new/gs-mains/2024/upsc-mains-2024-general-studies-paper-2.pdf)]. Calling the limit a soft guide is a coaching reading, not UPSC's wording.

UPSC does not publish model answers or marking schemes; candidates learn only their marks [[2](https://www.quora.com/Does-the-UPSC-release-the-Mains-answer-copies-of-the-toppers)]. No on-record interview with a named former examiner was found. Examiners reportedly mark against a confidential outline of key points and look for "factual accuracy, clarity, and a multi-dimensional perspective" [[3](https://politicsforindia.com/how-upsc-mains-copies-are-evaluated-in-real/)], but the source itself disclaims its account as hearsay.

## Question formats the site must ask for

| Format | Marks | Word limit | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| GS I–III, short | 10 | 150 | Printed instruction for Q1–10 [[1](https://www.vajiraoinstitute.com/assets/study-materials/pdfs/upsc-pyq/archive/new/gs-mains/2024/upsc-mains-2024-general-studies-paper-2.pdf)] |
| GS I–III, long | 15 | 250 | Printed instruction for Q11–20 [[1](https://www.vajiraoinstitute.com/assets/study-materials/pdfs/upsc-pyq/archive/new/gs-mains/2024/upsc-mains-2024-general-studies-paper-2.pdf)] |
| GS IV Ethics, theory | 10 | 150 | 2024 Section A [[4](https://indianexpress.com/article/upsc-current-affairs/upsc-essentials/upsc-mains-exam-2024-ethics-paper-analysis-9582198/)] |
| GS IV Ethics, case study | 20 | 250 | Secondary [[5](https://lotusarise.com/ethics-questions-upsc-mains/)] |
| Essay | 125 | 1000–1200 | 2024 Essay paper [[6](https://www.drishtiias.com/images/pdf/UPSC%20Mains%202024%20Essay%20Paper...pdf)] |
| Optional | 10 / 15 / 20 | not printed | Optional papers have eight questions, Q1 and Q5 compulsory [[7](https://r2.anantamias.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/geography-optional-2026-paper-i.pdf)] |

The grader reads "(Answer in N words)" from the question when it is present and lets it override the chosen format. The Lotusarise split of 125 marks per GS IV section does not add up (six case studies at 20 marks make 120), so only per-question marks are used. Optional-paper word counts are suggestions (about 150, 250 and 300 words for 10, 15 and 20 marks), because UPSC does not print them.

## How scores are distributed

Candidates consistently report that about 4/10 is average and 5–6/10 is good on a 10-mark question, with the best GS papers reaching about 140/250 [[8](https://www.quora.com/In-the-UPSC-CSE-how-many-marks-are-good-to-get-for-a-10-mark-question)]. A moderately good answer rarely gets more than 4 or 5 [[9](https://examprepspace.quora.com/https-www-quora-com-How-much-do-I-need-to-score-in-each-of-the-four-GS-papers-and-the-Optional-papers-to-qualify-in-UP)]. Toppers score around 50% [[10](https://www.quora.com/Why-do-UPSC-toppers-get-less-marks-like-30-to-40-in-the-GS-Mains-paper)].

The grader's levels therefore mean: **Off track** (does not answer the question), **Developing** (below the average answer), **Good** (an average-to-good exam answer, roughly 4–5 of 10), **Strong** (topper level, roughly 6 of 10 or more). "Good" is a high bar, not a default.

## Length

No source describes a formal cap or per-word penalty. Marks reportedly follow the number of distinct, relevant points against the key-point outline, and candidates describe roughly half a mark per distinct point [[11](https://www.quora.com/What-are-some-strategies-to-score-a-single-digit-rank-in-the-Mains)]. Padding does not help: one candidate reports that writing only what they knew, even below the limit, scored better than repeating points [[12](https://www.quora.com/How-many-marks-on-a-single-question-out-of-12-5-can-be-expected-by-UPSC-for-excellent-GS-answers-in-civil-services-Mains)].

Rules (ratio = words written ÷ word limit; Hindi answers get a 20% allowance on the limit because postpositions and auxiliaries are separate words, which is an inference to be calibrated on real scripts):

| Ratio | Effect |
|---|---|
| below 0.4 | Level at most Developing; the first fix is the length, stated with the actual numbers |
| 0.4–0.6 | Level at most Developing; a length fix |
| 0.6–0.7 | Level at most Good only if every other Good condition holds; Strong not possible |
| 0.7–1.3 | No length effect |
| above 1.3 | A fix to cut to the limit; Strong not possible |

The numbers in a length fix come from the word count, never from the model.

## Structure

Coaching sources agree on an introduction, a body organised by the question's parts and a forward-looking conclusion. Vajiram & Ravi gives 5–6 points over 2–3 dimensions with 1–2 examples for a 10-mark answer, and 6–7 points over 3–4 dimensions with 2–3 examples for 15 marks [[13](https://vajiramandravi.com/upsc-exam/upsc-mains-answer-writing-strategy/)]. Anantam IAS asks for a 25–35 word introduction that does not restate the question [[14](https://r2.anantamias.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/upsc-answer-evaluation-rubric.pdf)] and a conclusion that is forward-looking, not a restatement [[14](https://r2.anantamias.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/upsc-answer-evaluation-rubric.pdf)]. A missing conclusion is a common reason scores stall [[15](https://edukemy.com/blog/how-to-write-upsc-mains-answers-that-score-12-15-with-examples/)]. Subheadings are optional [[16](https://store.pw.live/blogs/upsc-exams/how-to-write-10-marker-answer-upsc-mains)].

Rules: the structure check must name the introduction sentence, the conclusion sentence and the body points. Code then checks that the introduction is in the opening, the conclusion is the closing sentence or paragraph and not the same sentence as the introduction, and that the body has at least as many distinct points as the norm (5 for 10 marks, 6 for 15, scaled for other marks). Structure can be "Good" only when all three are present and the body meets the point count. A single run-on paragraph with no conclusion cannot be "Good".

## The command word

Directive compliance is the criterion almost every source agrees on, and each directive has a typical failure. Discuss fails when it is one-sided [[13](https://vajiramandravi.com/upsc-exam/upsc-mains-answer-writing-strategy/)]; Analyse fails when it describes facts without reasons [[13](https://vajiramandravi.com/upsc-exam/upsc-mains-answer-writing-strategy/)]; Evaluate fails without a verdict [[13](https://vajiramandravi.com/upsc-exam/upsc-mains-answer-writing-strategy/)]; Critically examine fails as criticism without examination [[13](https://vajiramandravi.com/upsc-exam/upsc-mains-answer-writing-strategy/)]; Suggest fails when the answer spends its space on the problem [[17](https://www.studyiq.com/articles/upsc-mains-directive-words-2010-2026/)]. In a two-part question a strong first part does not make up for skipping the second directive [[17](https://www.studyiq.com/articles/upsc-mains-directive-words-2010-2026/)].

Rules: the directive check is given the failure mode for the question's command word and must say whether the answer commits it. The level can be "Good" only if the directive check is Good; "Strong" also needs the dimensions check Good.

## Content, evidence and facts

The consensus criteria are several dimensions in the body, specific evidence (schemes, reports, data, judgments, Articles) and accurate facts. Edukemy says breadth beats depth: 5–6 angles at moderate depth score better than 2 covered exhaustively [[15](https://edukemy.com/blog/how-to-write-upsc-mains-answers-that-score-12-15-with-examples/)]. Unacademy's guide splits marks 60% content and 40% presentation [[18](https://static.uacdn.net/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/06/15195426/ANSWER-WRITING-GUIDE-BOOK-1-1.pdf)]. Invented or irrelevant filler is not a genuine attempt [[19](https://www.insightsonindia.com/2026/06/25/upsc-mains-answer-writing-strategy-unconventional-rules-to-score-maximum-marks/)].

Rules: the existing claim checks against the Constitution stay. A serious factual error caps the level at Good; two cap it at Developing. Strong also needs the evidence check Good (at least one specific example, report, case or figure that supports a point).

## The level, in code

| Level | All of these must hold |
|---|---|
| Strong | every question part answered; ratio 0.85–1.3; structure Good; directive Good; dimensions Good; evidence Good; no serious error |
| Good | every question part answered; ratio at least 0.7 (0.6 with every other condition met); structure Good; directive Good; at most one serious error |
| Developing | the answer addresses the question but misses a Good condition |
| Off track | the answer does not address the question, or every part is missing |

The report says which condition stopped the next level up, in plain words, so a student sees why an accurate 69-word answer is "Developing".

## Formats judged differently

Ethics case studies are judged on applied decision-making: stakeholders, the value conflict, options weighed, a chosen and justified course of action, implementation and long-term measures [[20](https://www.vajiraoinstitute.com/blogs/mastering-upsc-gs-paper-4-case-study-answer-structure.aspx)], and solutions must be ones an officer could carry out [[20](https://www.vajiraoinstitute.com/blogs/mastering-upsc-gs-paper-4-case-study-answer-structure.aspx)]. Essays are not long GS answers; point-list essays tend to score average marks [[21](https://unacademy.com/content/upsc/upsc-mains-essay-writing-2026/)]. Strong essay scripts score about 125–140 of 250 for the paper [[22](https://vajiramandravi.com/upsc-exam/upsc-mains-essay-paper-analysis-2025/)].

Rules: until their own checks exist, the site offers Ethics case studies as a format and the report states that they are judged with the GS checks and that a dedicated case-study review is not yet available. Essays are not offered: the answer box takes 450 words, and an essay is 1000–1200. Hindi-medium answers get the same content rules and the 20% word allowance; there is no documented evaluation penalty for Hindi, although recommendations of Hindi-medium candidates for IAS and IPS fell from 27 in 2022 to 5 in 2024 [[23](https://indianexpress.com/article/education/upsc-cse-hindi-medium-candidates-10-recommended-for-ias-ips-in-2021-figure-fell-to-5-in-2024-10817527/)].

## Known gaps

The 2022, 2023 and 2025 papers were not verified one by one; the GS IV section split is unconfirmed; no first-hand examiner account was found; the Hindi word allowance and the point-count norms are practitioner conventions or inference. The test set must therefore include full-length answers at each level, and the level rule must be re-checked against examiner-marked answers once those exist.
